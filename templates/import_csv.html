Manual Historical Entry

For pre-app transactions without a CSV export, enter them one by one via Add Transaction.

Where to find your transaction history in Dime:
  1. Open Dime App → Assets → US Stocks
  2. Tap Dime Analytics → Annual Summary
  3. Note: Cash flow summary (buy/sell) and Dividend summary are separate tabs
  4. For per-trade history, contact Dime support for a broker statement
Priority — what to enter first:
  • All BUY transactions — needed for cost basis on any future sell
  • SELL transactions from 2024 onward — these generate assessable gains
  • Dividends from 2024 onward — assessable at receipt
  • Pre-2024 sell transactions — for completeness but not tax-assessable

Historical Exchange Rate Lookup

The app auto-fetches USDTHB from Yahoo Finance for recent dates. For older dates (2+ years back), fetch may be less reliable. Use these sources:

  • Bank of Thailand: bot.or.th → Statistics → Exchange Rates → Daily
  • Google: Search "USD THB [date]" for quick lookup
  • Kasikorn / SCB rate archives (if you used their transfer)
Consistency matters — if you use BOT rates for some transactions and bank rates for others, stick to one method. Record which method in the Notes field.
